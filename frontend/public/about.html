Krish AI: talk it out, in your own language

Krish AI is live and free at krishai.net. It is an AI saarthi from India for the moments when something is on your mind: exam stress, a career choice, pressure at home, money worries, or just a heavy day.

Start talking to Krish, free

Why people use Krish

  • It speaks the way you do. Write in Hinglish and Krish answers in Hinglish. Hindi gets Hindi, English gets English. You can also talk out loud.
  • Real answers, not just sympathy. Krish picks up what you actually said and gives one clear next step: what to study first, the exact words to say to a parent or boss, how to calm a racing mind.
  • Wisdom from the Bhagavad Gita. In the Gita, Krishna is Arjuna's saarthi, the guide who helps him see clearly in a hard moment. Krish shares a verse only when it truly helps, with its meaning in plain words. You can turn on a short daily verse at 7 am.
  • Small things that help. Breathe with me, a quick mood check-in, and with Pro, Krish remembers your last talk.
  • Study help too. Explain a chapter, quiz you before an exam, or plan your week.

What Krish is not

Krish is not a resume, PPT or document builder, even though it can make a deck or document when you ask. It is not a therapist or doctor either. If someone may be in danger, Krish always shows helpline numbers.
