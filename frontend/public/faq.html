Krish AI: questions and answers

Krish AI is live and free at krishai.net.

Is Krish AI live, or coming soon?

Krish AI is live today. It is free to use at krishai.net on your phone or laptop. Nothing to wait for and no waitlist.

What is Krish AI?

Krish AI is an AI saarthi from India. You can talk to it about whatever is on your mind, like exam stress, a career choice, family pressure, money worries or a bad day, in Hindi, Hinglish or English. It gives real answers and honest advice, and shares Bhagavad Gita wisdom when it truly helps. It also helps with studies and everyday questions.

Is Krish AI a resume, PPT or document builder?

No, that is not what Krish is for. Krish can make a slide deck or a document if you ask, but its main job is to be the friend you talk things through with, and to help you study and decide.

Can I talk to Krish in Hinglish?

Yes. Krish replies the way you write. Hinglish in English letters gets Hinglish back, Hindi in Devanagari gets Devanagari, and English gets English. It also speaks Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi and Urdu. You can talk out loud by voice too.

What is the Counsellor?

The Counsellor is the part of Krish you open when something is on your mind. It listens, picks up what you actually said, and gives you one clear next step. It has a Breathe with me exercise and a mood check-in. With Pro it remembers your last talk.

Where does the Bhagavad Gita come in?

Now and then, when a verse really fits what you are facing (fear, failure, anger, grief, a hard choice), Krish shares it with its meaning in plain words. It never preaches. You can also turn on a short daily Gita verse that arrives at 7 am.

Is Krish a therapist or doctor?

No. Krish is an AI friend, not a therapist, doctor or lawyer. If a message suggests someone may be in danger, Krish always shows helpline numbers. For a serious problem, please talk to a professional or someone you trust.

Is Krish AI free?

Yes, the Basic plan is free. Pro costs Rs 499 a month and Max costs Rs 1,299 a month, for more messages, memory of your past talks and stronger models.

Are my chats private?

Your chats are tied to your account and only you can see them. You can delete any chat. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Is there an app?

Open krishai.net in your phone browser and choose Add to Home Screen. Krish then opens like an app, and can send the daily verse as a notification.

Who makes Krish AI?
